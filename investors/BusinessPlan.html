3. The SQE Technical Infrastructure

Final Price = (Market_Base × Decay) + (Yield × Trajectory × Sentiment × 3)

3.1 SQE v4 Formula Pipeline

  • API-Football Data Ingest: Live match stats (goals, assists, cards, ratings) fetched per fixture → normalised into SQE input vector.
  • Performance Yield Calculation: Weighted stat contribution per event type with age multiplier and form window applied.
  • Cold Decay Engine: weeksCold counter per player — 0.95× price per inactive week prevents stale asset inflation.
  • Tournament Stage Multiplier: Group stage 1.0× → Final 3.0× — a WC Final goal generates 3× the SQE yield of a group stage goal.
