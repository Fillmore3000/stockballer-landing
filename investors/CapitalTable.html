Legal & Structural Notes

  • The ASA is a binding subscription for shares under English law. Pre-conversion, the £250,000 subscription sum sits as a liability on the balance sheet until shares are formally issued. Unlike a SAFE, the ASA is structured to comply with UK SEIS/EIS requirements.
  • ESOP should be issued via an EMI scheme (HMRC-approved) where eligible — gives employees significant tax efficiency vs. unapproved options.
  • The ASA converts to Ordinary B shares on the earlier of: (a) the next qualifying equity financing round, or (b) the longstop date — 6 months from the date of investment. The 6-month longstop is required to maintain SEIS eligibility. Conversion price is the lower of the £1.5M cap price or any priced-round discount. No discount is negotiated in this ASA.
  • SEIS relief (50% income tax credit + CGT exemption) available to qualifying investors. Seek HMRC Advance Assurance before closing.
  • Recommend founder shares have a 4-year reverse vesting schedule (12-month cliff) — standard investor expectation at pre-seed.
  • Drag-along, tag-along, pre-emption, anti-dilution and information rights to be formalised in a Shareholders' Agreement upon ASA conversion.
  • Series A projections in Table 3 are illustrative only and do not constitute a commitment or offer.
