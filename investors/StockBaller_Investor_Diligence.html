Section 6 — Real World Asset Architecture & Oracle Infrastructure

Performance Tokenization:
The RWA Architecture

What Makes Player Tokens Real World Assets

Player tokens are not purely speculative instruments. They belong to an emerging category called Performance-Backed Tokens — a subset of Real World Assets where the "real world" reference is an athlete's verifiable on-field performance, maintained on-chain through a cryptographically secured data pipeline.

Traditional RWA

Tokenized T-bills, real estate, gold. The underlying asset has a fixed, independently custodied value. The oracle reports a market price.

Example: USDC (fiat-backed), Ondo Finance (T-bills)

Performance Token (StockBaller)

The underlying "asset" is an athlete's career trajectory — quantified by the SQE formula into a deterministic fair value. The oracle reports match statistics, not a market price.

No custodied asset — value is formula-derived from real-world events.

The Real World Data Pipeline

REAL WORLD EVENT (goal scored, match played, red card) ↓ API-Football v3 → StockBaller backend fetches stats (verified source) ↓ SQE v4 formula → deterministic price calculation (auditable, reproducible) ↓ Admin key signs price payload → cryptographic proof of calculation ↓ PriceOracle.sol verifies signature → rejects any unsigned or tampered data ↓ On-chain price updated → permanent, immutable record ↓ Aerodrome DEX pool reads on-chain price → secondary market reflects real performance

Price Integrity: Three-Layer Validation

LayerMechanismWhat It Prevents
1. Data Source API-Football v3 — industry-standard provider used by sportsbooks globally. Results are publicly verifiable against official match records. Fabricated match data. Any discrepancy is publicly visible within minutes.
2. Formula Transparency SQE v4 is deterministic — given identical inputs it always produces identical outputs. Formula logic is open-source and auditable by any technical investor. Arbitrary price manipulation. Every price move is mathematically traceable to a specific real-world event.
3. On-Chain Immutability Every price update is permanently recorded on Base L2. Price change limits in PriceOracle.sol: max ±30% per update, 1-hour delay on changes >15%. Single-event manipulation. A hacked admin key cannot move prices beyond circuit breaker limits.

Section 11 — SQE v4: Core IP Technical Specification

SQE v4: The Formula
That Prices Every Token

The Sports Quantification Engine (SQE) is the proprietary algorithm that converts real-world match statistics into a deterministic, fair-value price for each player token. It is the core IP of the platform. Every token price, every revenue stream, every DEX pool reference — all trace back to this formula.

SQE v4 — Master Formula

Final Price = (Market_Base × Decay_Factor) + (Yield × Trajectory × 3) Where: Market_Base = Starting reference price for the player (set at token creation) Decay_Factor = Compound decay from inactivity: 0.95 per week with no match Yield = Performance score derived from this match's statistics Trajectory = Direction and strength of recent form trend (–1.0 to +2.0) × 3 = Amplification constant (calibrated on WC 2023 dataset)

Component Definitions

ParameterDerivationRangeNotes
Market_Base Assigned at token creation based on FIFA ranking, historical goals per 90, and international caps. Seeded from API-Football player profile data. £1.00 – £200.00 Lionel Messi, Erling Haaland tier = £150–200. Journeyman internationals = £1–10.
Decay_Factor 0.95 ^ weeks_since_last_match. Applied per player per weekly settlement. 0.95 (1 week) to 0.60 (10 weeks) Prevents token price inflation for benched/injured players. Solved the "stale token" problem in v1/v2.
Yield Weighted sum of match events: Goals (+3.0), Assists (+1.5), Clean Sheet GK/DEF (+1.0), Yellow Card (−0.5), Red Card (−3.0), Penalty Miss (−2.0), MOTM bonus (+1.0). –5.0 to +10.0 per match Weights calibrated against 2,400 WC 2022 player-match records. Hat-trick = +9.0 raw Yield.
Trajectory Exponential moving average of last 3 match Yield values. Positive form amplifies price rises. Negative form dampens recovery. –1.0 to +2.0 Prevents single-match manipulation. A one-off good game against poor recent form = modest, not explosive, price move.
Stage Multiplier Applied on top of Final Price based on tournament stage. Group: 1.0×. Round of 16: 1.25×. QF: 1.5×. SF: 2.0×. Final: 3.0×. 1.0× – 3.0× Creates non-linear tournament stakes. A goal in the World Cup Final moves the token 3× more than a group-stage goal.

Worked Example — Real Calculation

Step 1 — Decay_Factor: 0.95 ^ 0 = 1.0 (played this week, no decay) Step 2 — Yield calculation: Goals: 3 × 3.0 = +9.0 MOTM: +1.0 Yield = 10.0 Step 3 — Trajectory: Last 3 matches: [+6.0, +4.5, +8.0] → EMA = +1.72 Step 4 — Final Price: = (180 × 1.0) + (10.0 × 1.72 × 3) = 180 + 51.6 = £231.60 pre-multiplier Step 5 — Stage Multiplier (QF = 1.5×): = £231.60 × 1.5 = £347.40 final token price Price change: £180.00 → £347.40 (+93.0% in one match)
